USDC Reserve-Income Estimate (AQA / AQAv2)

USDC (+other pegged stablecoins) circulating on Hyperliquid L1 · DefiLlama, daily
This is the float AQAv2 reserve income scales off of — a balance, not trading volume. Drag to zoom · range buttons above chart.
ESTIMATE · off-protocol · not in revenue_daily

Hyperliquid's AQA (Aligned Quote Asset) program, upgraded to AQAv2, designates USDC as the primary margin/spot/perp asset on Hyperliquid. Under it, Coinbase classifies USDC held on Hyperliquid as "on-platform", collecting the reserve income it generates and paying ~90% of that income back to Hyperliquid (Coinbase keeps the remaining ~10%, net of the issuer's own costs — the official docs specify "cost-adjusted reserve yield revenue"). Per Coinbase's own AQAv2 activation announcement (2026-06-08), USDC reserves stood at $6.18B when the float was classified "on-platform" — ≈8% of USDC's global circulating supply at the time, and 95.06% of Hyperliquid L1's own stablecoin base. The chart above tracks this float live via DefiLlama (pegged-stablecoin supply on "Hyperliquid L1", USDC is ~95%+ of it) — $6.19B as of the latest data point below, a reasonable cross-check against Coinbase's own figure two months on. The scenario table uses this live number, not the frozen June figure. Validator-approved protocol-level accrual begins 2026-08-26 (30-day accrual intervals, paid 8 days after each interval closes), with the first Assistance Fund payment expected 2026-10-03. A future network upgrade will make AQAv2 mandatory for quote assets on HIP-4 and validator-operated perp markets, extending this beyond USDC/Coinbase. This income is routed to the Hyperliquid Assistance Fund, which executes open-market $HYPE buybacks — it is separate from, and additive to, tracked protocol fee revenue. No live feed exists for this — this is a manual, clearly-bounded estimate, not a tracked metric.

The 90% share and accrual mechanics are now confirmed by Hyperliquid's own docs (hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hypercore/aligned-quote-assets), corroborated by 19/26 validator approval and a $4.4B Circle→Coinbase USDC restaging on HyperEVM reported 2026-08-17 — stronger footing than the secondary-outlet-only framing (CoinDesk, CryptoBriefing, Bankless, Coinbase's activation post) this estimate started from. The prior Jun 8 vs. Aug 26 accrual-date disagreement is resolved: Jun 8 was Coinbase's own "on-platform" classification date (float existed, no protocol-level accrual yet); 2026-08-26 is when validator-approved AQAv2 accrual actually begins, with first Assistance Fund payment 2026-10-03 — math confirmed by the docs' own 30-day accrual + 8-day settlement mechanism. Confirmed via ASXN's own compiled JS that this income has no path into Hyperliquid's on-chain fee revenue at all.
