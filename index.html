USDC Reserve Income (AQA / AQAv2) — Actual Payouts & Run-Rate

USDC (+other pegged stablecoins) circulating on Hyperliquid L1 · DefiLlama, daily
This is the float AQAv2 reserve income scales off of — a balance, not trading volume. Drag to zoom · range buttons above chart.
ACTUAL PAYOUTS · HL ledger · not in revenue_daily, included in P/S incl. AQAv2

Hyperliquid's AQAv2 (Aligned Quote Asset) program: Coinbase/Circle remit ~90% of the cost-adjusted reserve yield on USDC held on Hyperliquid to the protocol. Per the official docs it is accrued daily at 0 UTC, settled in 30-day intervals (from 2026-08-26), and paid to the Assistance Fund 8 days after each interval closes — so it lands on-chain as one lump per month, as a system send of USDC from 0x5000…0000 to the Assistance Fund (0xfefe…fefe), which funds open-market $HYPE buybacks. DefiLlama does not count it, so it is absent from revenue_daily; the headline P/S adds the latest payout annualized (×365/30) on top of on-chain revenue.
